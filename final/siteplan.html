Site Plan : ChopLagos

A guide to Lagos Street Food and Local Dishes

Site Name

ChopLagos

The word "Chop" is a Nigerian Pidgin slang for "to eat" — a word used across lagos in everyday conversation. The name is short, easy to remember and signals that the site is about food specifically rooted in Lagos culture rather than reading a generic food blog.

Illustrative domain: choplagos.ng

Site Purpose

ChopLagos is a digital guide to popular street food and local dishes found around Lagos. The site helps residents and visitors discover what dishes exist, where they are commonly sold, how spicy they are, and a rough estimate of what they cost. Visitors can filter dishes by region or spice level, save the ones they want to try using Local storage and submit a dish suggestion or rating through a simple form.

Scenarios

  • I'm visiting lagos for the first time — what street food should I try, and how spicy is it going to be?
  • I enjoy spicy food — which dishes on this site are spiciest, and where can I find them?
  • I just found a wonderful dish i want to remember for later — can I save it without creating an account?
